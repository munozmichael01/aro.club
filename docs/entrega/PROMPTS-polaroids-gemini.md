# Prompts de polaroids para Gemini · primera tanda de 4

Van cuatro, no ocho, a propósito: son las cuatro que más estresan el
documento de la mesa. Si estas pasan, la receta sirve y pedimos las otras
cuatro con la misma base. Si fallan, cambiamos la base antes de gastar en el
resto.

Cada prompt es autónomo, se pega tal cual. Están en inglés porque el modelo
sujeta mejor las restricciones así, sobre todo las negativas.

**Formato:** cuadrado, 1:1. **Sin marco blanco**: solo el contenido
fotográfico. El marco de polaroid, el borde inferior ancho y la sombra los
pone Code en CSS, para que las ocho salgan idénticas y con el canto limpio.

**Cómo se revisa cada una**, antes de darla por buena: seis personas donde
toque, tres y tres, nadie vestido igual que otro, nadie que se parezca a
otro, ninguno con traje, y al menos dos conectando entre sí. Si falla una
sola, se rehace.

---

## 1 · La mesa desde arriba

> Overhead photo looking straight down at a round wooden table in a
> restaurant in Caracas at night. Exactly six people seated around it: three
> women and three men, all between 25 and 35, all of clearly different
> appearance from each other — different skin tones from light to dark brown,
> different hair, different builds. Latin American, Venezuelan. Each one
> dressed differently from all the others in relaxed casual clothes for a
> night out: an untucked shirt, a good t-shirt, a simple dress, jeans. No
> suits, no ties, no blazers, nobody dressed alike. Half-finished plates,
> wine glasses, beer bottles, bread, hands reaching across the table. Two of
> them are leaning toward each other mid-conversation, one is laughing.
> Nobody is sitting silent or staring at their plate.
>
> Shot on a phone by someone at the table, not by a photographer: imperfect
> framing, one person cropped by the edge, slight motion blur, visible grain,
> warm slightly washed colours, warm tungsten light from above. No studio
> light, no flash bouncing off faces. No brand logos, no readable text or
> signage anywhere, no watermarks, no white border or frame around the image.
> Square format.

## 2 · Las risas

> Photo of a restaurant table in Caracas at night. Two people in their
> late twenties laughing hard at something a third person just said: one has
> thrown their head back, the other is covering their mouth. They are clearly
> different from each other — different skin tone, different hair, dressed in
> completely different casual clothes, no suits or blazers. The person who
> spoke is in the foreground, out of focus, seen from behind over their
> shoulder. They only met tonight and it shows: it is the moment of
> recognising something funny in a stranger.
>
> Shot on a phone by someone at the table: imperfect framing, slight motion
> blur, visible grain, warm slightly washed colours, warm tungsten light.
> Faces partly turned away, nothing in sharp close-up. No brand logos, no
> readable text or signage, no watermarks, no white border or frame around
> the image. Square format.

## 3 · El brindis

> Close photo of six hands raising and clinking glasses over the middle of a
> dinner table in Caracas at night, caught at the moment of contact with a
> little motion blur. Wine, beer and one glass of water. The arms belong to
> different people: different skin tones from light to dark brown, some with
> bracelets, sleeves rolled up, one bare arm, one in a light linen shirt.
> Relaxed casual clothing only — no suit sleeves, no cufflinks, no ties, no
> formal jackets anywhere in the frame. Below, a wooden table with
> half-finished plates and crumpled napkins. No faces in frame, only forearms
> and hands.
>
> Shot on a phone by someone at the table: imperfect framing, visible grain,
> warm slightly washed colours, warm tungsten light from above. No brand
> logos, no readable text or signage, no watermarks, no white border or frame
> around the image. Square format.

## 4 · Contando algo

> Photo of a dinner table in Caracas at night. One person is telling a story
> with both hands up mid-gesture, seen from the side with their face partly
> turned away. Three people around them are listening, completely caught up
> in it: one leaning in, one with a hand over their mouth, one starting to
> laugh. Mixed group of women and men between 25 and 35, all visibly
> different from one another in skin tone, hair and build, each dressed
> differently in relaxed casual clothes for a night out. No suits, no ties,
> no blazers. Half-finished plates and glasses on the wooden table.
>
> Shot on a phone by someone at the table: imperfect framing, slight motion
> blur, visible grain, warm slightly washed colours, warm tungsten light. No
> brand logos, no readable text or signage, no watermarks, no white border or
> frame around the image. Square format.

## Un cambio que hay que hacerle al guion B

La polaroid 7 sigue siendo «la mesa ya vacía con las sillas movidas». Eso
contradice el documento de la mesa que acabamos de cerrar: vendemos que la
gente vino y se quedó, no el después. Hay que cambiarla, y la sustituta
natural es el momento que mejor cuenta el producto:

> **7 · Dos personas descubriendo que tienen algo en común**, una señalando
> a la otra, las dos sorprendidas, el resto de la mesa mirándolas.

Es una línea en el guion de Design, no toca los tiempos ni los giros.
